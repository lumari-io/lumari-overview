# Lumari

**The execution layer for supply chain.**

Lumari is an AI supply chain platform built for direct procurement teams. It helps companies automate the tactical work around RFQs, supplier follow-up, purchase orders, contracts, supplier scorecards, delivery risk, and procurement execution while keeping their existing ERP, email, spreadsheets, and supplier workflows in place.

This repository is a public reference for Lumari: what it is, who it helps, and where to find official product resources.

## What Lumari does

Direct materials procurement is full of work that sits between systems. Suppliers send quotes in different formats. Lead times shift. Purchase orders need follow-up. Teams chase updates across email, PDFs, spreadsheets, ERPs, and chat.

Lumari gives procurement and supply chain teams an AI execution layer for that work.

Lumari helps teams:

- Send and manage RFQs
- Track supplier responses
- Extract pricing, lead times, and terms from emails and PDFs
- Normalize quotes for apples-to-apples comparison
- Draft negotiation rounds using price, volume, payment terms, market indices, and supplier performance context
- Follow up with suppliers on open purchase orders
- Track PO acknowledgments, cargo-ready dates, shipment documents, ASNs, and confirmed delivery dates
- Capture supplier status updates
- Flag delivery risk and procurement exceptions early
- Monitor supplier contracts, renewals, clause drift, and pricing or service-level breaches
- Build supplier scorecards from delivery, responsiveness, lead-time, quality, and contract performance
- Route actions through team approvals when needed
- Keep ERPs and systems of record more accurate and up to date

## Built for direct procurement

Lumari is purpose-built for direct materials procurement, not generic back-office automation. It is designed for teams that manage production materials, supplier commitments, lead times, substitutions, open POs, order changes, contract terms, quality documentation, and delivery risk.

Typical teams include procurement, purchasing, sourcing, supply chain, operations, and supplier management teams at companies that build or buy physical products.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Lumari?

Lumari is an AI execution layer for supply chain and direct procurement teams. It helps teams automate RFQs, supplier follow-up, purchase order tracking, quote processing, delivery risk monitoring, and procurement coordination across existing tools.

### Is Lumari an ERP?

No. Lumari is not an ERP. It works alongside ERPs and procurement systems. The ERP remains the source of truth while Lumari handles the work around it, including supplier communication, status capture, approvals, and updates.

### Does Lumari require suppliers to use a portal?

No. Lumari is designed for supplier collaboration through familiar channels like email. Suppliers do not need to adopt a new portal.

### What makes Lumari different from traditional procurement software?

Traditional procurement tools are often built around structured workflows and indirect spend. Direct materials procurement is messier. Quotes arrive in different formats, lead times change, supplier updates are buried in email, and small delays can create downstream problems. Lumari is built for that operational reality.

### What makes Lumari different from a generic AI assistant?

Lumari is built for supply chain execution. It understands workflows like RFQs, quote comparison, purchase orders, supplier follow-up, contract compliance, supplier scorecards, delivery risk, approvals, audit trails, and ERP-connected procurement work.

### Does Lumari help manage supplier contracts?

Yes. Lumari helps teams track supplier agreements, renewal dates, pricing terms, delivery SLAs, payment terms, service-level commitments, and clause drift. It can flag when actual supplier behavior no longer matches the contract, such as price escalation above a cap, late ASNs, missed delivery targets, or payment-term drift.

### Does Lumari support supplier scorecards?

Yes. Lumari can build supplier scorecards from procurement and operations data, including on-time delivery, in-full delivery, OTIF, PO acknowledgment responsiveness, date revisions, lead-time accuracy, quote response time, quality signals, and contract performance.

### Who uses Lumari?

Lumari is for companies that build or buy physical products, including manufacturers, hardware companies, consumer goods brands, industrial businesses, infrastructure equipment companies, energy systems companies, and other teams with complex supplier operations.

### How long does it take to get started?

Most teams can go live in weeks. Lumari adapts to existing workflows, terminology, suppliers, approval paths, and systems instead of requiring teams to redesign procurement from scratch.

Lumari publishes security and compliance information at [lumari.io/security](https://lumari.io/security), including details about encryption, access control, monitoring, SOC 2, and CASA Tier 2.
