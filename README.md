# Lumari

**The execution layer for supply chain.**

Lumari is an AI platform for direct procurement and supply chain operations. It helps teams monitor supplier work, coordinate across systems, and take action from sourcing to delivery without forcing suppliers into a new portal.

[Website](https://lumari.io) | [Blog](https://lumari.io/blog) | [Security](https://lumari.io/security)

## What Lumari does

Supply chain teams spend a lot of time chasing updates, reading supplier emails, checking RFQs and purchase orders, and moving information between ERPs, spreadsheets, inboxes, and collaboration tools. Lumari gives those teams an AI execution layer that works across the tools they already use.

Lumari can help with:

- Supplier collaboration and follow-up
- RFQ intake, quote comparison, and purchase order workflows
- Order monitoring, delay detection, and exception handling
- Supplier email processing for quotes, confirmations, shipping updates, and risks
- Approval-based actions, including supplier replies and ERP updates
- Audit trails for agent actions, approvals, edits, and decisions

## Built for direct procurement

Lumari is purpose-built for direct procurement teams that manage production materials, supplier commitments, open purchase orders, lead times, substitutions, and delivery risk.

Teams use Lumari to reduce manual touchpoints while keeping their existing process, suppliers, and systems of record in place.

## How Lumari fits into your stack

Lumari works alongside existing systems rather than replacing them. It connects company knowledge across ERPs, email, Microsoft Suite, Google Workspace, communication tools, spreadsheets, documents, and supplier records.

The platform adapts to:

- Existing approval chains
- Standard operating procedures
- Naming conventions
- Escalation paths
- Supplier relationships
- Internal controls and audit requirements

## Why teams use Lumari

- No supplier portal required. Suppliers can keep working through email and familiar channels.
- Live in weeks. Lumari maps to existing workflows instead of requiring a long system migration.
- Human approvals where needed. Teams decide which actions can run automatically and which require sign-off.
- Full visibility. Actions are logged and searchable.
- Works with the ERP. Lumari layers on top of systems of record rather than forcing rip-and-replace.
- Improves with feedback. Agents learn from corrections, approvals, and operating patterns over time.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Lumari?

Lumari is an AI execution layer for supply chain and direct procurement teams. It helps monitor work, coordinate supplier communication, and move procurement operations forward across existing tools.

### Is Lumari an ERP?

No. Lumari works alongside ERPs and other systems of record. It helps teams act on the work around those systems, including email, RFQs, POs, supplier updates, approvals, and exception handling.

### Does Lumari require suppliers to use a portal?

No. Lumari is designed for supplier collaboration through familiar workflows, including email. Suppliers do not need to adopt a new portal for Lumari to help procurement teams.

### What makes Lumari different from a generic AI assistant?

Lumari is built for supply chain execution. It understands procurement workflows like RFQs, purchase orders, supplier follow-up, delivery risk, approvals, audit trails, and ERP-adjacent work.

### Who is Lumari for?

Lumari is for supply chain, procurement, purchasing, operations, sourcing, and supplier management teams that want to reduce manual coordination without replacing their core systems.
